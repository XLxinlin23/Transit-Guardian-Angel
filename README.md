# Commute Guardian

•A centralized operations and incident response tablet app for Rachel -- fixed-schedule

commuter

Tampines to Raffles Place, EWL,

leaves 07:40, at her desk by 08:45

Same trip for four years; checks no app on a normal day. Five minutes is noise,

fifteen costs her a meeting. Interrupt her only when it matters, in one line. the navigation bar on the button: home, preference, disruption alert



The interface needs to be for phone, and the colours mostly white, with dark and bright blue and green
